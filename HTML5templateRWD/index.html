Eris Distillery is a queer-owned spirits brand in Texas, crafting premium liquors from ethically and sustainably farmed Red Hard Winter Wheat. Rooted in integrity and community, Eris reinvests locally to nurture a vibrant, healthy future.

Beyond distilling, the brand channels the bold, electric spirit of queer nightlife—where self-expression, resilience, and celebration collide. Each bottle embodies the pulse of the clubs that inspire it: unapologetic, inclusive, and alive with possibility.
